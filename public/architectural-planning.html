From a room brief to coordinated drawings

We start by discussing the family’s needs, parking, stairs, daylight and privacy. The concept review gives you a chance to compare arrangements before the detailed work proceeds. Ask for the agreed design scope, review stages and drawing list in your proposal.

Floor plans describe how the rooms fit together. Elevations let you review the frontage and finishes. Working drawings then coordinate dimensions and construction details with the structural and service designs. The issue schedule should make clear which drawings are ready for pricing, submission and site execution.
