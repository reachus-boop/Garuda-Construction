What does 1,000 sq ft mean?

Plot area and built-up area are different measurements. For example, a 500 sq ft ground floor plus a 500 sq ft first floor gives 1,000 sq ft of total built-up area. Your agreement should state how the area is measured and which spaces are counted.

What can change the final estimate?

  • The total built-up area and how it is measured.
  • The package, material brands and finish choices.
  • Soil and foundation requirements at the site.
  • Whether approvals, utility connections, compound walls or external works are included.
  • Payment milestones, exclusions and any changes made during construction.

How to compare construction quotes

Compare proposals using the same area, material quality and work scope. Ask each builder to list included items, excluded items, brands, payment stages and the process for approving changes. That makes the numbers easier to understand before you decide.
