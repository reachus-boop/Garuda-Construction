House Construction Cost in Thiruvallur

Compare sample budgets for 1,200 and 1,500 sq ft homes, and find out what to ask before you compare builder quotes.

By Garuda Construction · 17 November 2026

Garuda’s residential packages for Thiruvallur list rates from ₹2,089 to ₹2,689 per sq ft. At those rates, 1,200 sq ft of billable construction area works out to ₹25,06,800–₹32,26,800 before any separately priced items. The exact quote depends on your drawings, site conditions and written scope.

Calculate the base cost for your floor area

Use the floor area that the contractor will bill for. For a duplex, ask for an area statement for each floor and written treatment of stairs, balconies and covered parking. A 1,200 sq ft plot can have a different billable construction area.

What can change the estimate after a site visit?

For a plot in Tiruvallur town or nearby Kakkalur, Manavala Nagar or Periyakuppam, send the site location, plot dimensions and a photo of the approach road. These let the team ask the right questions before pricing work:

  • Foundation scope: Ask what excavation, filling and foundations the quote assumes and how any engineer-directed changes would be priced.
  • Access and levels: Show how materials reach the plot and where the site sits relative to the road. A visit helps identify access, drainage and ground preparation the quote should address.
  • Work outside the house: Confirm whether the compound wall, gate, sump, paving and drainage connections are in the package or listed separately.
  • Fees and finish choices: Ask for applicable taxes, approval and utility charges, and upgrades to be priced or identified separately in the quote.

How do I budget for an independent 3 BHK?

Two homes with three bedrooms can have different construction areas. Start with the layout you need, including bathrooms, parking and shared space.
