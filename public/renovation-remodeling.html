Plan a renovation around your existing home

Start by telling us which rooms need work and what you want to improve. For a house in Thiruvallur, Kakkalur or Manavala Nagar, site photos and a short description help us prepare for the visit. Include recurring leaks, earlier repairs and whether the family will remain in the house during work.

What we discuss at the site visit

The discussion covers access, retained finishes, existing services, the proposed room changes and any investigation needed before the scope can be finalized. We can then discuss an itemized proposal with quantities, material choices, assumptions and work stages.

For terrace waterproofing or dampness, describe when the problem occurs and where it appears. The proposed treatment should follow an assessment of the source. Keep the repair scope, checks, access requirements and warranty terms together in the proposal.

Considering an additional floor?

A qualified structural engineer needs to assess the existing building and proposed work. Bring any available approved plans and structural drawings. The first-floor addition guide explains the records, access decisions and construction questions to prepare.
