Cost Calculation FAQs

Questions About Rates & Quotations

Everything you need to know about how we estimate house construction costs in Thiruvallur.

100% Fixed! Once we inspect your land, verify soil bearing capacity, and sign your construction agreement, the price per sq.ft is strictly locked. Even if cement or steel market prices rise during the construction period, we absorb the difference with zero price escalation to you.

Built-up area includes all enclosed living spaces (bedrooms, hall, kitchen, dining, pooja room, and bathrooms) measured from outer wall to outer wall. Car porch and covered balconies are billed at discounted rates (₹1,200/sqft and ₹1,000/sqft), while open terrace has zero charge except for parapet wall.

You never pay large upfront sums. You only pay for each milestone after our site engineer completes it and you personally inspect it. If a stage is not finished to standard, you do not release the next tranche. This gives you 100% financial peace of mind.

Yes, absolutely! If you choose the Classic Package (₹2,150) but want luxury Kohler diverters or Italian marble flooring in the living room only, we adjust the bill transparently for that specific upgrade difference without forcing an entire package tier shift.
