The Journey

7 steps from plot to housewarming.

Planning two floors? See the G+1 construction timeline for Chennai for a checklist of decisions and site checks at each stage.

  1. 01

    Plot Survey & Soil Testing

    Physical site inspection, boundary verification, and soil bearing capacity test to calculate precise foundation depth.

  2. 02

    2D Vaastu Plan & 3D Elevation

    Interactive architectural design sessions with Principal Architect Harish Balaji until you approve every room and elevation angle.

  3. 03

    DTCP / CMDA Sanction

    Complete documentation, structural drawings submission, and liaison for government building plan approval.

  4. 04

    RCC Foundation & Structure

    Heavy isolated footings, anti-termite treatment, plinth beam, columns, and concrete slab casting with strict 21-day curing.

  5. 05

    Masonry & Concealed Services

    High-precision brickwork, concealed CPVC plumbing lines, Havells electrical conduits, and two coats of cement plastering.

  6. 06

    Finishes & Woodwork

    Vitrified flooring, waterproof bathroom tiling, teak door frames, window installations, and multi-coat painting.

  7. 07

    Deep Clean & Handover

    120-point quality audit, electrical load testing, water flow checks, deep cleaning, and ceremonial key handover.
