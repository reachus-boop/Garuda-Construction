Water leakage is the #1 complaint with careless contractors. Here is how we prevent it permanently:

  1. We apply a 2-coat chemical polymer waterproofing membrane (Fosroc / Dr. Fixit) directly on the concrete slab.
  2. We conduct a mandatory 48-hour water ponding test to verify zero seepage.
  3. We lay cool roof weathering tiles with neat 1:40 slope towards oversized rainwater outlet pipes so water never pools on the roof.
