Plan the building around its business use

For a shop, office or commercial complex in Thiruvallur, tell us how customers, staff and deliveries will use the site. The initial brief should record the intended occupancy, floor areas, access needs and services the business requires. Those decisions help the design team coordinate the proposal.

Bring an operating brief to the first meeting

Describe working hours, equipment, expected users, storage and future tenant requirements. Ask how the proposal will coordinate circulation, accessibility, fire-safety requirements, parking and service routes. The professional handling submissions should confirm the requirements for the site and proposed use.

Define what the construction proposal covers

Agree the drawings, structural work, finishes, building services and fit-out responsibilities before comparing prices. Record specialist equipment, owner-supplied items, testing and handover documentation. A phased project also needs a clear boundary between each completed stage and the space still under construction.

Contact the Thiruvallur office with your location and use brief. If you are comparing procurement arrangements, the contract responsibility guide can help structure the discussion.
