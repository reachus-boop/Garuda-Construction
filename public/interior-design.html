Prepare a room brief for your Thiruvallur home

Bring a floor plan, room photos and a list of what you want to store. Tell us how you cook, work, receive visitors and use each bedroom. For a new home, coordinating interiors while the services are being planned helps the team agree appliance locations, plumbing points and lighting positions together.

What to agree for a modular kitchen

List appliances, utensils, pantry needs and washing activities. Review cabinet dimensions, internal fittings, countertop choice and access for maintenance. Ask for the proposed material specification and the treatment of items you will supply yourself.

Storage and lighting for everyday use

For wardrobes, discuss hanging space, shelves, drawers and loft use with the people using the room. For living spaces, agree seating, media equipment and lighting activities before finalizing joinery. Keep the approved dimensions, finish samples and hardware choices with the execution proposal.

Coordinate installation with other work

For an occupied house, agree which rooms remain available and how installation will be sequenced. If changes include existing plumbing, wall finishes or room layouts, coordinate them with the renovation scope. For a new house, bring the interior brief to your architectural plan review.
