G+1 House Construction Cost in Chennai: 30×40 Plot Example

Estimate the cost of a G+1 house on a 30×40 plot in Chennai. See a worked 1,400 sq ft estimate, compare two sample quotes, and check common extras.

By Garuda Construction · 17 November 2026

A 30×40 plot measures 1,200 sq ft, but that is only the land area. If your ground floor has 750 sq ft of built-up area and your first floor has 650 sq ft, the two floors total 1,400 sq ft. At an illustrative rate of ₹2,300 per sq ft, the base construction estimate would be ₹32.2 lakh. The actual quote depends on how the builder measures billable area and what the rate covers.

Start with the floor areas on your plan

G+1 means a ground floor and one floor above it. A narrow frontage, space for parking and the layout of stairs can mean the two floors have different footprints. Ask your architect for a floor-by-floor area statement from a plan that fits your site. The example above uses assumed floor areas for budgeting. Have the design team confirm what can be built on your plot before you price the work.

Give every builder the same drawings. Ask each one to show how it counts external walls, stairs, balconies, the covered car porch and any terrace headroom. Ask whether each feature is included in the billable area, priced as a separate item or excluded. A quote using carpet area and a quote using the outside dimensions of each floor can produce very different totals.

What changes when you add 200 sq ft?

Illustrative budgets at ₹2,300 per sq ft
Billable areaCalculationBase estimate
1,200 sq ft1,200 × ₹2,300₹27.6 lakh
1,400 sq ft1,400 × ₹2,300₹32.2 lakh
1,600 sq ft1,600 × ₹2,300₹36.8 lakh

In this example, adding 200 sq ft increases the base estimate by ₹4.6 lakh at the same rate. A design change can also affect the structure or specifications, so ask for a revised quote before committing. Use the construction cost calculator to try your own area and package, and see the Chennai construction cost guide for broader rate context.

Compare the price for the same scope

Suppose Builder A quotes ₹2,250 per sq ft for 1,400 sq ft, then adds ₹1.25 lakh for covered parking and ₹75,000 for external drainage. The comparable amount is ₹33.5 lakh. Builder B quotes ₹2,350 per sq ft for the same area with those two items included, for ₹32.9 lakh. With the same specification and tax treatment, Builder B is ₹60,000 lower in this example.

Ask for a written breakdown of these items before comparing totals:

  • Ground conditions: What foundation work is allowed for? How is any change priced after the soil investigation?
  • Finishes: Which brands or allowances cover tiles, sanitary fittings, doors and windows? What does an upgrade cost?
  • Outside the house: Check the compound wall, gate, sump, drainage connections and paving against the drawings.
  • Separate costs: Ask for taxes, approval fees, utility deposits and owner-supplied interiors to be itemised where applicable.

Take these details to your first consultation

Bring your plot dimensions, road-facing side, location, parking needs and room list for each floor. If you already have drawings, bring the area statement and any quote you want to compare. See the G+1 construction timeline to plan when decisions and payments will fall.
