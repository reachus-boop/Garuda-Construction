1. Why We Are the Trusted Construction Company in Vanagaram

Neighborhoods in Vanagaram—such as Sivabhoomi, Adayalampattu, and areas bordering the Cooum river valley—frequently encounter elevated water table levels during Northeast monsoons. Inferior foundation construction in this belt leads to basement dampness, efflorescence on interior plaster, and rusted rebar.

Garuda Construction deploys advanced sub-surface moisture mitigation protocols across Vanagaram:

  • Engineered Deep Footings with Crystalline Additives: Concrete for column footings and plinth beams is mixed with crystalline waterproofing compounds that react with moisture to permanently seal capillary pores.
  • Double-Layer Elastomeric Basement Tanking: Heavy-duty polymer bitumen membranes applied to the exterior face of all foundation brickwork and underground sumps.
  • High-Grade Fe 550D TMT Rebar: Certified Tata Tiscon and JSW corrosion-resistant steel bars provide high ductility and long-term protection against ground moisture degradation.
